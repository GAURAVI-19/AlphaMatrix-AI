### Slide 2: The Core Challenge: "Black-Box AI"
* High-stakes AI decisions (hiring, attrition risk, performance reviews) lack transparency.
* Opacity leads to algorithmic bias, legal liability (EU AI Act), and employee mistrust.
* Need for a system combining prediction accuracy with mathematical explainability and mandatory human governance.

### Slide 3: The Solution: AlphaMatrix 8-Layer Architecture
* **Layer 1-2**: Multi-Domain Ingestion & Live Glassmorphic Parameter Tuning
* **Layer 3-4**: XGBoost Prediction Engine & SHAP / LIME Feature Attributions
* **Layer 5-6**: Ethical AI Firewall (Score >= 70%) & Human-in-the-Loop Approval Queue
* **Layer 7-8**: Security Audit Logs, ISO Decision Certificates, and Role-Based Dashboards
